IPv6

The router takes IPv6 from the provider on its internet-facing interfaces: an address (SLAAC or DHCPv6) and, if the provider delegates one, a prefix. Each LAN zone's interface gets a /64 network of that prefix, which its devices configure themselves from (SLAAC; ipv6.lan_zones chooses the zones, all of them by default). Each also gets a stable network of the router's own (ULA), where the router answers DNS whatever the provider gives. The rules decide what the LAN reaches over IPv6, as over IPv4; nothing new comes in from the internet but through a pinhole (ipv6.pinholes). The webUI and SSH answer over IPv6 only with management.ipv6, on the stable address.
