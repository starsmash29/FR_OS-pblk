IPv6 on the WAN

The router takes IPv6 from the provider on its internet-facing interfaces: an address (SLAAC or DHCPv6) and, if the provider delegates one, a prefix for the LAN. Only the router itself uses it for now: nothing is routed over IPv6, and every IPv6 packet to the router is dropped but what the WAN needs: neighbour discovery, the provider's router advertisements, DHCPv6, ICMPv6 errors and ping.
