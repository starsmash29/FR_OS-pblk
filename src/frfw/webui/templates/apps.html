Applications

Recognises which apps clients use from the names they look up through the router's resolver and, optionally, the TLS server names (SNI) the XDP filter sees -- nothing is decrypted. A "hit" is one lookup or one TLS connection, a rough activity signal, not traffic volume. Apps are blocked by the resolver answering "no such domain" for all of their names.
